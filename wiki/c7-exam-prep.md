# C-7 license exam prep

Fran is preparing for the California CSLB C-7 (Low Voltage Systems) license: two tests, Law and
Business and the C-7 trade test. Target: 80% or better on practice before test day.

## Key facts (sources: cslb.ca.gov, checked 2026-10-07)
- Application fee $450 (non-refundable); license fee after passing $200 sole owner, $350 company.
- $25,000 contractor's bond before the license issues.
- Retake: likely $100 per test, paid to PSI (unconfirmed); wait 21 days; pass both within 18 months.
- No official passing percentage; about 72% is commonly cited by prep sites.
