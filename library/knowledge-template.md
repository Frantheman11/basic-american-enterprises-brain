# Blank knowledge page

Create a subject page under `wiki/` with these fields:

- Title and business/scope.
- Status: draft, confirmed or superseded.
- Last checked date.
- Current understanding, with facts distinguished from assumptions.
- Limits, unresolved contradictions and references to task IDs if follow-up is needed.
- Sources: original path or URL, date captured, and who confirmed an interpretation.

Keep structured business facts in `config/business.json`; link to the relevant field rather than duplicating its value. Update the index in the same change.
