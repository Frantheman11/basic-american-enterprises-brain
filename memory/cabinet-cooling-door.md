Decision: Fran will fabricate a new cabinet door to carry the supplemental VoltAire HIX heat exchanger.
The door has a built-in cavity (pocket) that houses the heat exchanger. The pocket bulges OUTWARD from the cabinet (confirmed 2026-10-08), to keep inside space clear.
Proposal phases (decided 2026-10-08): three phases. 1 Prototype and discovery (survey, design, 2 prototype doors: 1 ADTRAN + 1 Nokia; changed from 4 on 2026-10-08), fixed price $69,000 all-in, 2 Field test and refinement, 3 Production and rollout.
Phase 1 pricing inputs (Fran, 2026-10-08): HIX unit $5,000 each, door $1,200 each, labor $125/hr. Door installation goes through a licensed partner until Fran's C-7 is in hand. Working estimate ~$59,000 for Phase 1 (184 labor hrs, 15% materials markup, 10% contingency).
Scope: BOTH cabinets, ADTRAN and Nokia (confirmed 2026-10-08).

Facts from the manuals that shape the door:
- ADTRAN Crossover Enclosure: 44.6 x 24.5 x 24.5 in; existing heat exchanger (55 W/°C) sits on TOP, not a door; front and rear doors lift off (hook at bottom, 2 tamper-proof screws, padlock hasp); DC fuse F4 is unused.
- Nokia outdoor cabinet: existing heat exchanger is on the front door; rating not given; PTS sits back-to-back 1.5-2 in behind it; rectifiers 2 x 1000 W.
- HIX55: 99 W/°C, 35.5 x 17.6 x 8.5 in, 47.5 lb. Only model that gets the ADTRAN to ~150 W/°C total.
- Decision (Fran, 2026-10-08): the new heat exchanger is AC powered. HIX55 AC: 3.5 A at 115 VAC (HIX055M) or 1.9 A at 230 VAC (HIX055N). AC models have no VoltAire fan alarm board (48 VDC only) and use a cord grip, not Molex. HIX25 is DC only, so not an option. Price assumed unchanged at $5,000; confirm with VoltAire.

**Why:** the original design report assumed door-mounted units and wrong ADTRAN dimensions.
