# Talking points with Spencer: G Fiber site walk

Company: Basic American Networks · Wednesday 2026-10-21 · Internal notes, not for G Fiber.
Facts from `memory/cabinet-cooling-door.md` and `memory/g-fiber-proposal.md`.

## The job in one line
A new door for each cabinet with a pocket that bulges **outward** and houses a VoltAire HIX55 heat
exchanger (AC powered). Phase 1 = 2 prototype doors: 1 ADTRAN, 1 Nokia.

## The heat exchanger the door must carry (HIX55)
- 35.5 x 17.6 x 8.5 in, 47.5 lb
- AC power cord with a cord grip (not Molex)

## Measure on each cabinet (ADTRAN and Nokia)
- Door opening: width, height, and the existing door's thickness
- Hinges: type, side and position; how the door comes off
  (ADTRAN doors lift off: hook at the bottom, 2 tamper-proof screws, padlock hasp)
- Seal: gasket type and where it seats, so the new door seals the same way
- Lock and hasp position; tamper-proof hardware to reuse or match
- Clearance in front of the cabinet for an outward pocket about 9+ in deep (sidewalk, curb, walkways)
- Photos and model labels of both cabinets

## Cabinet-specific points
- **ADTRAN** (44.6 x 24.5 x 24.5 in): its current heat exchanger is on top, not the door, so the
  door is a clean slate. Front and rear doors: which one gets the pocket?
- **Nokia:** the current heat exchanger is already on the front door, and the PTS sits only
  1.5 to 2 in behind it. Check the inside clearance and whether the old unit comes off or stays.

## Ask Spencer
- Can the hinges and frame carry the heavier door plus 47.5 lb? Does the hinge need upgrading?
- Material and gauge (aluminum or steel), and the finish to match the existing cabinet
- Airflow: where the pocket's vents go so intake and exhaust are not blocked
- How the HIX mounts in the pocket, and how a tech removes it for service
- Cord path from the door into the cabinet (hinge-side loop, cord grip, strain relief)
- Grounding and bonding the door
- Weather sealing around the pocket
- Cost per prototype door and lead time for 2 doors
- Rough volume pricing (4-6, 7-24, 25-99, 100+ doors): the Phase 3 tiers depend on it
- Check our working assumptions: door about $1,200 list, $500 to mount the HIX in the door,
  $500 to install the door at the cabinet

- Door installation goes through a licensed partner until your C-7 is in hand.
