Re: Proposal 4344, Supplemental Cooling Retrofit for Outdoor Fiber Cabinets

Dear Biswajit,

Thank you for the chance to look at cooling for your outdoor fiber cabinets. Please find our proposal enclosed.

Your ADTRAN and Nokia cabinets are set to carry about 500 W more electronics. Their existing heat exchangers can only bring the inside down toward outdoor air temperature, and the extra load will push that gap wider. We propose adding a second, independent, AC-powered VoltAire HIX55 heat exchanger to each cabinet. The existing cooling stays in place.

The new unit sits in a custom-fabricated cabinet door with an outward pocket. The pocket protects the unit from weather and tampering, leaves the inside of the cabinet free for equipment, and gives the cabinet a factory-built look. On the ADTRAN cabinet, we estimate the inside will run about 9.7°C above outdoor air at 1,500 W, against about 18°C today at 1,000 W.

We suggest starting small, in three phases:

  • Phase 1, Prototype and discovery: survey one ADTRAN and one Nokia site, engineer both doors, and build and install two working prototypes. $69,000, fixed price, all-in.
  • Phase 2, Field test and refinement: monitor both prototypes through hot, full-load conditions and give you a go / no-go recommendation.
  • Phase 3, Production and rollout: doors for the remaining cabinets, with indicative per-cabinet pricing in the proposal.

Each phase starts only with your written approval.

One point to raise early: the new unit runs on AC power, so it does not load your DC rectifiers, but the planned 1,500 W of electronics leaves both cabinet types without spare rectifier capacity. We will review the power plan, including the AC feed for the new unit, with you in Phase 1. Any power upgrade is outside this price and would be quoted separately.

The proposal is valid until 7 November 2026. As a next step, I suggest a short call to walk through it and pick the two Phase 1 sites.

Thank you for considering Basic American Networks. I look forward to working with you.
