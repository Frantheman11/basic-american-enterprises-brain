# Fran: start here

Your second brain is this folder. Claude reads it every time you open it, so it remembers your
business, your people and your rules.

## Open it (once)

1. Move this folder (**Fran-Second-Brain**) into **Documents**.
2. Open the **Claude** app. Click **Code** at the top.
3. Click **Select folder** and choose **Documents → Fran-Second-Brain**.
4. When Claude asks for permission to read or edit files in this folder, click **Yes** /
   **Allow**. It only works inside this folder.

## Then type these, one at a time

Type the words in **bold** exactly. Claude leads from there and asks you questions. Answer in
your own words; "yes", "no" and "not quite" are fine.

| # | Type | What happens | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **install my brain** | Claude checks what it knows about Basic American Enterprises and asks you to confirm or correct it, one question at a time | 30 to 45 min |
| 2 | **file my inbox** | Put your investor deck, rep agreement, territory map and a few of your emails in the `inbox` folder first. Claude reads them and files the facts | 10 to 20 min |
| 3 | **map my week** | Claude asks how a normal week goes and lists what it can take off your plate | 15 min |
| 4 | **back up my brain** | Claude walks you through a private backup on your GitHub and adds Sam so he can help | 10 to 15 min |

You can stop at any time. Next time, type **install my brain** and Claude picks up where you
left off.

## Every day after that

- **good morning**: what is on your plate, who to follow up with, what changed.
- **remember that ...**: tell Claude anything worth keeping. It files it.
- **draft an email to ...**: Claude writes it in your voice. You send it.
- **what do I know about ...**: a person, a company, an investor, a state.

## If something looks wrong

Type what you see, for example "that's not right, Louis is the ...". Claude fixes the file.
Stuck? Call Sam.
