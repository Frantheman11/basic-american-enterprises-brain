# Notes from Fran's call with Sam, 2026-10-01

Source: a 44-minute call about the new website. Facts to confirm with Fran during setup.

## What Basic American Enterprises is, in Fran's words
- **A fund.** "When you buy in, I get to decide how much of your investment goes to which company. I'm the fund manager." Investors invest in the fund, never in a single company or a rep.
- **An incubator with distribution built in.** Every portfolio company gets lead generation and distribution, plus accounting, marketing and strategy. "It's a rare thing to have an incubator actually set up distribution for companies."
- **A master agency.** 50 key people in 50 states, three regional offices (West, Central, East) with a regional director each. Reps are established, independent and commissioned, and close their own leads. Models: ACS, Telarus, Sandler Partners.
- **Every portfolio company runs on a Custom Tailored Agents CRM.** Basic American Intelligence is the rep entity that contracts with Custom Tailored Agents.
- **The family of brands.** Basic American Enterprises is the parent. Basic American Networks (telecom reps) and Basic American Intelligence (AI reps) are rep categories under it.
- **Portfolio today:** iPost Sports, IoT Data Group, Basic American Networks, Custom Tailored Agents, Block Battery, Clear Insights AI, BlackMP. "That's not the long-term plan, that's just where we are right now."
- **Cross-pollination:** an investor may have a cousin who should be a rep; a rep may have a cousin who should invest.
- **The map:** Michael built it: three regions, each state clickable; a click should list that state's reps.
- **Leads go to the companies, never to the reps.**
- **Rep sign-up stays off the site:** a form, then a call on Fran's calendar. Screening happens on the call.
- **The line:** "Smart money invests in smart American businesses." Always "smart money" and "smart companies".

## The website decision
- One site, investor first. The map and the structure one level down, as proof for investors.
- Rep landing pages per category (Networks, Intelligence) later.
