### Commercial Property Services

Developed with Building Owners & Managers in mind, Basic American Networks is successfully partnering and supporting property owners and managers to help them implement appropriate tech amenities such as high capacity Wi-Fi, Multi Media Boardroom Systems, DAS for optimal Cell Phone coverage and analog POTS lines for elevator controls and HVAC while meeting the needs and providing superior service to the tenants.

A growing trend in the commercial real estate market is the implementation of a comprehensive plan for edge compute, multimedia and security into multi-tenanted office property. Owners and managers are beginning to understand that their building’s communications system is the single most important amenity the property offers to new tenants.
