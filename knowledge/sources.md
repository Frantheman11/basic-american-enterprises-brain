## How this package was written

The folder conventions and operational guidance are original starter guidance informed by practical second-brain work, internal study of Professor Glitch's second-brain and skill-library ideas, and the public documentation above. The raw/wiki/index/log loop, corrections routed to their actual source and shared references instead of copied business facts are design principles used here.

This package does not contain Professor Glitch's videos, transcripts, internal study corpus or a republished knowledge base. It carries no claim of endorsement or certification by Professor Glitch or Anthropic. The precise folder taxonomy, task-list policy, maintenance budgets and support procedure are template choices, not Anthropic platform requirements.

Business pages begin blank. Their sources must come from the owner or identified evidence during installation. Do not use these structural guides as evidence for a business claim.
